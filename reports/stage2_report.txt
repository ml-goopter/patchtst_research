section 17 - volatility validation, PatchTST vs GBM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
aligned on 109,240 rows common to the multi-task run and src/volatility.py (109,240 multi-task rows)
competitors are CPU-only, fitted per fold on the F2 features with the same folds,
purging and train-only normalization; static_median is the fold's TRAIN median.
All models regress log volatility and exponentiate, so all report a median-like
point forecast and none gets a mean-vs-median advantage. One asymmetry remains:
the multi-task checkpoint is selected on validation return NLL (PLAN.md section
20), not on volatility loss, while every competitor is selected on its own task.
Competitor predictions are clipped at the fold's train 0.1% quantile of realized
volatility, since persistence can forecast exactly zero and QLIKE is a variance
ratio. The clip never binds for the transformer: its lowest prediction is 0.0017.
